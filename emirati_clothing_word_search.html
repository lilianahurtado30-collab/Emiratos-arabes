Learn about the clothing

  • Kandura: a long traditional robe worn by many men.
  • Abaya: a long, loose outer garment worn by many women.
  • Ghutra: a traditional headscarf.
  • Sheila: a scarf worn by many women.
  • Hijab: a head covering worn by some Muslim women.
  • Bisht: a traditional cloak worn over the kandura.
  • Ghafiya: a small cap worn under a headscarf.
  • Nikab: a face veil worn by some women.
  • Mishlah: a traditional outer cloak.
  • Turbante: a wrapped head covering.
